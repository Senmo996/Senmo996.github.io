Kent-based Distributional Proxy Hashing (KDPH) represents semantic classes as distributions on a hypersphere. Their directional variance accommodates conflicting label correlations while maintaining stable semantic directions. A loss incorporating the Cayley transform supports orthogonal geometric parameters during training.
