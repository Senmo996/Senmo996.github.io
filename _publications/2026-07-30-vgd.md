Verifier-Guided Decoding (VGD) uses a lightweight verifier to assess whether emerging object mentions are supported by visual evidence. Its Intrinsic Grounding Signature captures distributed attention patterns. When a mention is judged risky, VGD rolls back the KV cache, suppresses the object and its synonyms, and regenerates the affected continuation.
