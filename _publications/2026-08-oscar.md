Online Self-Calibration (OSCAR) addresses the mismatch between externally generated supervision and a vision-language model's own perceptual capabilities. It uses discriminative verification to construct preference data through Monte Carlo Tree Search and dual-granularity rewards, then iteratively updates the model with Direct Preference Optimization.
