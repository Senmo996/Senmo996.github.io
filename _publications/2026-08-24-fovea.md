FOVEA builds reusable visual memory and selects a bounded subset of evidence for each draft state. A cumulative-mass rule adapts the amount of retrieved evidence, which is fused into the draft representation through a gated residual correction before the language-model head. This design supports changing visual demands while preserving the autoregressive cache structure.
