关于站主
======

欢迎来到我的主页！我目前是中国科学院大学信息工程研究所计算机应用技术专业的博士生，师从[吴大衍](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~dayan)和[王伟平](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~wwp)。在此之前，我于2024年在长安大学获得自动化专业学士学位。我的研究兴趣主要聚焦于**智能体、强化学习和信息检索**。

About me
======
Welcome to my homepage! I am currently a Ph.D. student in Computer Application Technology at the Institute of Information Engineering, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by [Dayan Wu](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~dayan) and [Weiping Wang](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~wwp). Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Automation from Chang'an University in 2024. My research interests primarily focus on **AI agents, reinforcement learning, and information retrieval**.
